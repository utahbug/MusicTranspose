Evidence and limits

The physical reports share a 402×650 viewport, 376px score width, 79px score top and 102px footer. Good: 437px page budget, .57 scale, 13 systems, 7 pages. Bad: 362px budget, .62 scale, 15 systems, 8 pages. A 575px temporary viewport produces exactly the latter result.

At fixed geometry, fresh Full → Melody → Full had identical source printNewSystemXml / printNewPageXml flags, NewSystemAtXMLNewSystemAttribute and rendered system geometry. No source-break leak was reproduced.

A focused mobile-WebKit test renders during a temporary 75px-shorter layout viewport, then restores the real viewport and dispatches only visualViewport.resize. Before the fix, the stale 362px frames and .62 engraving persist; afterward the fresh 437px / .57 / 7-page layout returns exactly. This proves a missing-event-handling defect that reproduces the supplied measurements. It does not prove the precise event sequence on the physical phone; physical confirmation remains required.

Fix

No new pagination policy, scaling threshold, source-break edits, or song exception. Tablet/desktop handlers remain unchanged. URL-gated diagnostics v2 add scroll offsets and current calculated height for comparison with stored frame height. Service-worker shell v212 preserves the existing separate score cache.
