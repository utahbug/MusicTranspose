PDF continuous flow verification

2026-10-04. Screen presentation only. Service worker v231. Physical iPhone confirmation remains required.

Implementation

  1. Continuous and Auto-scroll use independent vertical bounds containing every nonwhite raster pixel, plus 0.8% of raster width as safety padding (15 pixels at 1800px). Adjacent source frames have a 12 CSS pixel seam. Existing horizontal bounds and internal spacing remain unchanged. Blank pages are conservatively retained.
  2. Footer detection uses the loaded PDF.js document's extracted text, never OCR. A candidate must begin with explicit legal wording, contain a dated copyright or clear rights/permission statement, and every trailing line must have recognizable legal wording. Credits, scripture, verses and musical instructions reject relocation.
  3. A candidate must lie in the lower half, occupy at most 18% of page height, be separated from prior ink by at least 1.5% of page width, and have every trailing raster pixel accounted for by its extracted text boxes. Later music, graphics, unextracted lines, rotated text or ambiguous material leave the notice in place. False negatives are intentional.
  4. The original page canvas is never edited. Existing overflow frames crop the continuous body. Only a short full-width strip is copied for each accepted footer, preserving original pixels. Distinct or repeated notices are retained in source-page order; no deduplication. No duplicate full-page canvases are retained for this feature.
  5. Page Turns restores the existing authored treatment synchronously. Annotation suspends continuous cropping and hides relocated copies. Original page coordinates and annotation storage are untouched.
  6. Printing still serializes original source canvases; export still returns PDF.js source bytes. No source PDFs were changed. Dark mode applies the same inversion to relocated strips. Page labels remain, and relocated notices have accessible labels; hidden strips are not exposed.
